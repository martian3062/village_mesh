VILLAGE MESH V2.5 - IMPLEMENTATION AND ACCEPTANCE
Implementation updated: 24 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata)
Authored scene date remains 23 October 2026.
Source plan: Village_Mesh_V2_5_Punjabi_TinyAI_Plan_20261023.txt

This is a living-village prototype on the V2 map. It is not accepted as a
finished photorealistic Punjabi population or complete eight-milestone release.
Every person, job, outfit and parcel ownership field is fictional/inferred.

M1 WORLD / CHARACTER CONTRACTS
Implemented: eight stable identities, individual capabilities, daily windows,
four demonstration parcels, crop date, three road-profile records, explicit
64-feature/48-action schema, masks, reservations and deterministic fallback.
The 39 unimplemented action outputs stay masked.

M2 COMPLETE ACTIVITY
Implemented runtime: native character movement on authored graph, work/return,
rain interruptions, roof shelter, task completion and event memory.
Pending: locally reviewed wardrobe and consented recorded voice; final movement
and contact animation acceptance. Current character art is procedural prototype.

M3 CLOTHES / FARMS / ROADS
Implemented prototype: field boundaries, crops/residue/soil, farm access track,
shade structure, stable outfit selections and a passage-controlled vehicle.
Pending: measured cross-sections, locally verified drainage/field appearance,
skinned modular wardrobe, cloth LOD/collision tests, tractor/trolley swept-path
and entry/exit animation. Runtime overlay does not rebuild V2 geometry/HLODs.

M4 TINY POLICY
Trained/exported 121,520-parameter policy, exact initializer audit and hashes.
Synthetic held-out teacher agreement 99.65%; zero invalid actions after masks.
PyTorch/ONNX maximum absolute error 0.0000152588 on the export fixture.
No behavioural advantage over rules established; rules remain default, learned
ranking is opt-in and gated by inference parity/freshness/current validity.
Unreal and packaged runtime results must be read from recorded check reports.

M5 PUNJABI
Implemented draft subtitle choices, Roman/English fields, world-state details,
Gurmukhi font pin/licence, full-shaping widget, UTF-8 byte-length contract.
Not accepted: native-speaker wording, tone, pronunciation or voice recordings.
Intent model is NOT trained/deployed; no macro-F1 claim or hidden language API.

M6 COMBINED PASS
Implemented weather responses, task/passage reservations, actor restore,
versioned local persistence, and bounded CPU inference scheduling.
Pending polished human contact/cloth animations, spatial recorded audio,
complete road/vehicle physical fidelity and locally reviewed work actions.
Recorded 30-minute run: 71 generic activities across six residents, four tractor
legs. Mehar and Jas completed no activity in that scenario; their completion
coverage remains open. The stability pass does not accept all eight routines.

M7 OPTIONAL MICRO LANGUAGE MODEL
Architecture counts are executable and audited. No decoder weights are trained
or deployed; the default build does not claim open-ended Punjabi conversation.
This optional experiment is intentionally outside release functionality until
training data, held-out language evaluation and native review exist.

M8 RELEASE EVIDENCE
Local engine/package checks are recorded under Saved/VillageMeshV25 and the
published Docs/V25 evidence. Fresh-machine and final shipping acceptance remain
open; local Development packaging does not establish either.
Recorded: 25 Python tests, three daylight and three rainy standalone traversals,
rendered rules/learned checks, actual missing-asset fallback and a continuous
1800-second rendered session passed. All final runs share one executable hash.

REQUIRED EVIDENCE BOUNDARIES
Frame wall time is not separate CPU/GPU timing. Whole-adapter memory/temperature
include other applications. NullRHI is simulation-only. Synthetic imitation
accuracy is not Punjabi comprehension, local fidelity or superiority to rules.
No unreviewed source asset, voice or local-person claim is accepted by these tests.
